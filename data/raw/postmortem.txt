# Postmortem: Payment Service Outage — 2023-07-14

## Incident Summary
On 2023-07-14, the payment authorization service (Service X) experienced a partial outage lasting approximately 25 minutes. During this time, payment requests either failed or experienced severe latency.

The incident was detected automatically via monitoring and resolved by the on-call SRE through a service restart and a temporary configuration change.

---

## Impact
- Duration: 25 minutes
- Affected services:
  - Payment authorization API
  - Checkout flow
- User impact:
  - Increased 5xx errors
  - High latency (>5 seconds)
  - Failed payment attempts for a subset of users

---

## Detection
The incident was detected by automated alerts:
- Alert: SERVICE_X_HIGH_LATENCY
- Alert: SERVICE_X_DOWN

Observed signals:
- Steady increase in memory usage
- Sudden OOM kill event
- Spike in error rate and request latency

---

## Root Cause
A memory leak in Service X caused unbounded memory growth under sustained traffic, eventually leading to an out-of-memory (OOM) crash.

Contributing factors:
- No alerting on abnormal memory growth rate
- Memory limits configured too high
- Lack of automated restart or circuit breaker

---

## Resolution
The on-call engineer performed the following actions:
1. Restarted Service X to restore availability
2. Increased the service memory limit temporarily
3. Monitored the service after restart to ensure stability

Service availability was fully restored after the restart.

---

## What Went Well
- Alerts triggered without customer reports
- Runbook steps were clear and effective
- Service recovery was fast once action was taken

---

## What Went Wrong
- Memory leak was not detected early
- No heap dump or profiling data was captured
- Monitoring focused on absolute memory usage, not growth trends

---

## Action Items
